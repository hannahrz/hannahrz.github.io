OPERATIONS RESEARCH · ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE运筹学 · 人工智能

Ruihan Zhou 周睿涵

Learning to make better decisions under uncertainty.研究不确定性下的学习与决策。

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Management Science and Information Systems, Guanghua School of Management, Peking University, advised by Prof. Yijie Peng.我是北京大学光华管理学院管理科学与信息系统系的博士研究生，导师为彭一杰教授。

My research lies at the intersection of operations management, stochastic optimization, and artificial intelligence. I study how organizations can use AI to improve decisions and coordinate people, information, and resources under uncertainty. My current work focuses on human–AI workflows and expert capacity, as well as probabilistic forecasting and dynamic resource allocation for new products and AI services. This work builds on my research in simulation optimization, ranking and selection, and sequential decision-making.我的研究结合运营管理、随机优化与人工智能，关注组织如何利用人工智能改善决策，并在不确定性下协调人员、信息与资源。目前，我重点研究人机协同工作流与专家资源配置，以及面向新产品和 AI 服务的概率预测与动态资源配置。此前在仿真优化、排序与选择及序贯决策方面的工作，为这些研究提供了方法基础。

From April to October 2026, I am a visiting scholar in the Department of Industrial Engineering and Decision Analytics at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, working with Prof. Xiaowei Zhang. I completed my undergraduate studies in the Department of Information and Computational Science, School of Mathematics and Statistics, Wuhan University in 2022.2026 年 4 月至 10 月，我在香港科技大学工业工程与决策分析系访学，合作导师为张晓炜教授。2022 年，我本科毕业于武汉大学数学与统计学院信息与计算科学系。

Diagnosis and Recovery in AI Workflows with Scarce Expert Capacity

Ruihan Zhou, Jiheng Zhang, and Yijie Peng

How should scarce expert attention be allocated in multistage AI work? I study the joint design of diagnostic requests, expert dispatch, and recovery decisions.当多阶段 AI 工作流中的专家资源有限时，如何协调诊断请求、专家调度与诊断后的恢复决策？

02
COLD-START FORECASTING冷启动预测

Cold-Start Probabilistic Forecasting of New-Product Trajectories

Ruihan Zhou, Zishi Zhang, Jinhui Han, Yijie Peng, and Xiaowei Zhang

Probabilistic life-cycle forecasting for new products with little or no sales history, using product features, reference products, and conditional diffusion models.结合产品特征、参考产品与条件扩散模型，研究在缺少或没有销售历史时，如何预测新品生命周期及其不确定性。
